Privacy Policy — Simple Todo

Application: Simple Todo  ·  Package: com.cidao.simpleapp
Last updated: 12 November 2026

In one sentence: Simple Todo does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your own device.

1. Who we are

Simple Todo is developed and published by an individual developer ("we", "us"). You can contact us at the developer email address shown on our Google Play store listing.

2. What data the app processes

Simple Todo processes only the task text that you type into the app. This data is written to the app's private internal storage on your device (an Android SharedPreferences file that no other application can read).

We do not process, request or access any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number or any account identifier
  • Location data (precise or approximate)
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files or microphone
  • Device identifiers, advertising ID or IMEI
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics
  • Payment or financial information

3. Network access

The app declares no internet permission and contains no networking code. It is technically incapable of sending your data to us or to any third party. There is no account system, no cloud sync and no backup to a server.

4. Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK and no third-party SDK of any kind. The only bundled runtime component is the Kotlin standard library, which performs no data collection and no network access. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your tasks remain on your device until you remove them. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • deleting individual tasks inside the app, or
  • using Android Settings → Apps → Simple Todo → Storage → Clear data, or
  • uninstalling the app.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no deletion request needs to be sent to us.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Security

Your tasks are protected by Android's application sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading this app's private storage on a device that is not rooted.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app starts processing any data, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

9. Contact

For any privacy question about Simple Todo, contact the developer email address listed on the Google Play store page for this app.

This policy applies to the Android application com.cidao.simpleapp distributed through Google Play.
